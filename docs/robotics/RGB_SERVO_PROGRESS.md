The new network outputs two bottle keypoints, visibility and a foreground mask. Calibrated triangulation estimates position and vertical axis. It does not infer absolute yaw of the nearly rotationally symmetric bottle. Simulator segmentation, body points and saved physical states are training/scoring sources only; the observer's `observe` method accepts RGB images and fixed camera calibration.

The initial consistency rule required all three confident views, at most 1.5-pixel reprojection residual, and the known 103 mm keypoint spacing within 6 mm. The later two-view and rigid-fit revisions are recorded below. Rejecting uncertain views improves accepted accuracy but reduces coverage. Both quantities are reported. No rejected state is silently omitted from the denominator.

## Corrective action implementation and current selection

The experimental controller now uses live RGB bottle points during approach/descent, a neural Cartesian-to-joint mapping, measured joint feedback, explicit skill phases and the existing separate physical monitor. It never calls an IK solver or reads simulator object poses to generate actions. After closure, the carry uses the observed grasp offset and robot proprioception; it does not visually replan the carried path. Encoder feedback detects an empty gripper. The earlier image-based post-lift refusal and its failures are retained in previous reports.

The first nominal exposed trial at seed 2026095102 passes after replacing 20 Hz target steps with smooth 200 Hz motor updates. A calibrated 0.75 N / 0.1 second base-height force shifts the bottle 12.07 mm without tipping. With the same disturbance, the v3 live-image trial passes at 0.52 mm placement error while the initial-image-frozen trial fails at 10.01 mm. Same-proprioception counterfactual actions differ. This is an exposed development result, not a broad robustness claim.

V4 adds 4,096 independent procedural states with exposed right-arm relay poses (151.16 seconds, 0.994 GiB VRAM). V5 adds 640 frames from the five replayed recoveries, completing the declared **30,000 cumulative Adam steps including the motor model**. V5 improves average development perception but regresses a right-arm physical case; it is retained and rejected for selection. V4 with two consistent views and a 0.35-weight RGB exponential filter passes the exposed right-arm placement at 4.11 mm. Its final exposed push pair is 0.75 mm live/pass versus 10.02 mm frozen/fail.

The frozen v4 perception test, seed 2026095412, accepts 225/231 present states, rejects all 25 absent states and reaches 3.180 mm p95 / 6.117 mm maximum error. It still misses the unchanged 3 mm gate. All 18 physical development trials then finish: **2/6 nominal, 4/6 pushed live, 4/6 pushed frozen**. Known reach refusals remain in the denominator; the four successful push pairs show no success-count advantage for live images in this set. This candidate is not promoted.

The separate [rigid-geometry refinement](experiments/rgb-servo-rigid-observer-v1.json) uses the known 103 mm keypoint spacing with frozen V4 weights and no extra neural training. It reduces original development p95 to 2.134 mm at unchanged coverage. Its fresh frozen perception seed 2026095413 accepts 233/236 present states and no absent states out of 20, with **1.938 mm p95 / 5.182 mm maximum** accepted error. This passes the unchanged 3 mm gate. An OpenVINO repeat on the same images matches these results; it is a parity check, not a second independent test.

Physical development identified a fixed-jaw approach margin smaller than measured positioning error. A declared 4 mm clearance change passes three exposed regressions. The complete selected OpenVINO development suite then passes **4/6 nominal live, 4/6 pushed live and 1/6 pushed frozen**; two pre-motion refusals remain in every denominator. No final evaluation outcome informed these changes.

The [final selection](experiments/rgb-servo-final-evaluation-v1.json) froze source, observer, motor and IR hashes before all **48 final trials**. On seeds 2026095301–5312, results are **2/12 nominal live, 1/12 nominal frozen, 3/12 pushed live and 0/12 pushed frozen**. Seven seeds are refused before movement. Other failures include missing RGB, descent timeout and placement outside the unchanged 8 mm threshold. Only seed 2026095309 passes both undisturbed controls; its pushed live result is 1.53 mm/pass versus frozen 11.34 mm/fail. This is narrow paired correction evidence, not broad robustness.

**The experiment is complete and not promoted.** [All outcomes and source snapshots](evidence/rgb-servo-v1/README.md), [frozen weights](../../models/bottle_servo_v1/README.md), compact training inputs and exact generation recipes are packaged separately. The 30,000-step budget is exhausted; these final seeds are now exposed. Further development needs a new bounded protocol and new final seeds, while retaining these failures. Both networks have FP32 OpenVINO export parity on this AMD PC; actual Intel execution remains open.

Raw datasets and failed candidates are retained in `.run/`; they are not automatically published or substituted for the selected models. Dataset/export scripts check actual free space before each batch, episode/shard and periodic writes, retaining 10 GiB plus expected output.

## V2 routing experiment

The separate [V2 protocol](experiments/rgb-servo-bottle-v2.json) reserves new training/development/final seeds and leaves V1 unchanged. Its 156-point kinematic comparison finds 103 neural acceptances and 104 solver acceptances. Only one neural refusal has a solver solution. The middle goal can be placed at low height but the original high transport pose has no solution in this probe. A later exposed-offset diagnostic also finds no solver solution at its 19 refused transport probes. These findings do not prove physical unreachability, but they do not justify more motor training yet.

Revision 1 chooses lift and transport heights separately and can route a table-supported relay. Exposed training seed 2026095605 completes both legs and places at 3.31 mm error. The other first four training seeds include an invalid overlap, occlusion, preflight refusal and post-lift route failure; all are retained. Revision 2 adds fresh visual checks before align/lower and three possible shared points. Its exposed trials reveal fresh-image failures during carry and another route refusal. No thresholds are relaxed.

Both allowed controller revisions completed all four conditions on six declared development seeds (24 cases each). R1 passed 3/6 nominal live, 0/6 nominal frozen, 3/6 pushed live and 1/6 pushed frozen. R2 passed 2/6, 0/6, 2/6 and 1/6 respectively. R1 was selected from these development results and [frozen](experiments/rgb-servo-final-evaluation-v2.json) before exposing V2 final seeds 2026095801–5812.

All **48 V2 final trials** finish: **6/12 nominal live, 2/12 nominal frozen, 5/12 pushed live and 1/12 pushed frozen**. Three seeds are refused before motion in every condition. Live table-supported relays complete both legs on two nominal and two pushed cases, but broad coverage remains poor. Only one seed passes both undisturbed controls; its pushed pair is 4.02 mm/live-pass versus 17.73 mm/frozen-fail. Different V1/V2 final seeds prevent treating their raw counts as a controlled improvement.

V2 misses its unchanged promotion gate and remains outside the browser. [All outcomes, both revisions and diagnostics](evidence/rgb-servo-v2/README.md) are packaged. The auditor checks all 48 reports, source/model hashes and 27,241 synchronized state frames. Neural weights remain the frozen V1 package; no extra training was used. V2 final seeds are now exposed and cannot inform further selection.

## V3 offline motor refinement

The separately declared [V3 protocol](experiments/rgb-servo-bottle-v3.json) tests gripper-position and axis losses on the same 4,056 training labels. Independent MuJoCo checks validate the training-only differentiable geometry. The 6,000-step RTX 4070 fit selects its final checkpoint on the original exposed kinematic development set: 361/362 accepted points, 0.564 mm p95 error, versus 355/362 and 1.084 mm before refinement. It misses the unchanged 0.500 mm p95 gate and stops without any physical trial or OpenVINO export. All allowed steps are consumed; the reserved physical development/final scenes remain unexposed.

[Both checkpoints, every per-point score and exact training sources](evidence/rgb-servo-v3/README.md) are preserved. CPU checkpoint reloads reproduce every acceptance decision and the failed gate. This improves the offline fit, but establishes no additional physical coverage and does not change the selected dinner baseline. The full training-contract suite now passes 15 tests, including independent geometry and gradient checks.
